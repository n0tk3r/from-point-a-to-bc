Acts and beats

Beats in different chains can be done in any order. The gate needs all of them. Violet is a cutscene, cyan a puzzle, magenta the gate.

Scenes

Each scene in its era's colours. Cyan boxes are clickable areas. The magenta box is where the lead can walk.

Era palettes

Every era fills the same slots: eight sky bands, two silhouettes, three grounds, dark, light, two features. The time colours on the right never change.

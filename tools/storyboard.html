Acts and beats

Beats in different chains can be done in any order. The gate needs all of them. Violet is a cutscene, cyan a puzzle, magenta the gate.

Maps

Each act's places, laid out by the ways between them, from the same files the game reads (each scene's edges, its exits and its spawn marks): what a scene's bottom edge leads to is drawn below it, its top edge above it, its left edge to its left and its right edge to its right. A solid line is a way out at an edge of the picture, with what the label line says over it; a dashed line is a door or a thing to click (what its label says, when a script there names the place); a diamond marks a way that is there only some of the time (its when); a violet arrow leaves the map for another act's place. Under each map, every way, and where it arrives.

Scenes

Each scene as it is when the story first gets there: its painted backdrop with its cut-outs laid over it and its people in place (a scene with no painting yet is sketched in its era's colors). Cyan shapes are clickable areas. The dashed magenta outline is where the lead can walk; solid magenta patches are ground something stands on, to walk around. A yellow line is the base of a painted cut-out: people whose feet are above it are drawn behind that cut-out. All the numbers are pixels on the 800x600 picture.

Era palettes

Every era fills the same slots: eight sky bands, two silhouettes, three grounds, dark, light, two features. The time colors on the right never change.

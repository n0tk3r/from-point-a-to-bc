Play the gameStoryboardSprite viewerHow to add music

To add a track: export a WAV from your music program, from the start to a few seconds past the loop's end, and put it in music-src/ named after the track (egypt.wav replaces the Egypt theme; any other name needs scenes:). Beside it, a text file of the same name says where it loops (bpm: 84, intro: 4 bars, loop: 32 bars). Then run python3 tools/music/add_music.py and come back here: Hear the seam plays the last seconds of the loop going round into its start.
